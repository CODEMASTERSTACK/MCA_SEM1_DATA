ResumeOS

What is ResumeOS?

In simple terms, ResumeOS is an intelligent, automated resume builder app for your phone. Instead of just giving you a blank, passive template to fill out manually, it acts like a personal career coach and ghostwriter combined. You give it your basic history, and it does the heavy lifting of figuring out exactly what companies want to read.

Resume preparation is broken in three specific ways

Each one is measurable on its own, and each one compounds the other two for the same student, at the same deadline.

Time scarcity
45–120 min

Preparing for exams like the CMA alongside a full course load leaves no room for admin work. Manually tailoring one resume, cross-referencing a job description, swapping projects, rewriting a summary, still takes 45 minutes to 2 hours. Near a deadline, that overhead means fewer applications go out, not more effort put in.

The ATS black hole
6–7 sec scan

A recruiter spends only 6 to 7 seconds on a first look, and before a human ever sees the resume it has to clear an Applicant Tracking System. Candidates are left guessing at terminology, and a multi-column layout can break the parser outright. Qualified applicants get filtered out before anyone reads their work.

The subscription trap
$24–30 / mo

Most builders let a student spend an hour filling in every detail, then lock the finished PDF behind a monthly plan at the very last step, often auto-renewing at $24 to $30. It is not just a cost problem. It is a trust problem, at the exact moment the student needed to hit download.

Three distinct kinds of friction: a time cost, a technical barrier, and a pricing trap. A tool that only fixes one of them still leaves a student stuck on the other two.

ResumeOS: One Application, three fixes

Less a template, more a ghostwriter that already knows the candidate's work. Connect your history once; the system handles the reformatting, the keyword-matching and the exporting on every application after that.

01

Frictionless portfolio integration

GitHub links directly into the platform. Repos, README documentation and detected tech stacks convert straight into professional project bullets, so nothing gets retyped by hand.

02

Intelligent semantic matching

Paste in a job description and the edge pipeline returns three things: a synthesized skills profile for that role, an algorithmic percentage match against the candidate's synced projects, and bullets written in a strict Action + Context + Tech stack + Metric formula.

03

The micro-transaction economy

Pay only at the moment of export: ₹5–10 for a single tailored PDF, or ₹50 for a 10-export bundle. No recurring plan sits in the background waiting to be cancelled.

Why not just use ChatGPT, or Zety?

Candidate impact

Only about a quarter of Indian resumes clear ATS screening today. A properly tailored application roughly doubles the interview rate. ResumeOS enforces that exact tailoring by default, every time, for the cost of a bundle instead of a rigid quarterly plan.

Institutional impact

Faster tailoring means more high-quality applications leaving the university, which moves campus placement statistics. The TPO dashboard gives placement officers live visibility into exactly which skills and keywords their students are missing against real job descriptions.

Pilot math

1,000 students purchasing a single ₹50 bundle is ₹50,000 in high-margin revenue, at close to zero customer acquisition cost through campus distribution alone.

Resources requested

Strategic mentorship and an institutional endorsement from the E-Cell, plus a small technical and legal talent addition to harden the build ahead of the wider beta.

Risk, named and mitigated

Hallucinated metrics
A JSON self-repair layer catches schema failures and forces a correction before compiling. Every bullet still passes through a human-in-the-loop editor before the PDF is generated.
Points-economy spoofing
Credit claims never trust the device clock. Every ledger update is validated against external UTC time (timeapi.io, worldtimeapi.org).
Data privacy
A single request recursively purges every subcollection and the auth record on account deletion. An in-house SLM is the long-term path to full data sovereignty.
